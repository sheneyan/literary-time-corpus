# Data rights policy

## Current release status

This repository does not yet publish a literary excerpt dataset. The root MIT
License applies to project-authored software and documentation; it does not
apply automatically to third-party literary text or source ebooks.

## Separate rights layers

Each future release must distinguish at least:

1. copyright in project-authored software;
2. copyright or database rights in the corpus selection and arrangement;
3. copyright in each literary work;
4. copyright in the specific edition, translation, introduction, annotation,
   or other editorial material used as a source; and
5. provider terms and names or trademarks associated with source files.

A source provider's eligibility statement is evidence for a recorded decision,
not a declaration that a work is unrestricted worldwide. In particular,
Project Gutenberg primarily evaluates status under United States law and tells
users outside the United States to check the law where they are located.

## Publication gate

Before any corpus artifact is published, the project must:

- define a versioned target-use profile;
- complete separate United States and China-mainland assessments for the
  initial target-use profile;
- fix the exact source file and its content hash;
- preserve evidence for the work and edition decision;
- complete human review of the excerpt and time interpretation;
- define terms for project-authored dataset elements; and
- generate a machine-readable attribution and rights report.

Records that are unreviewed, ambiguous, restricted, or insufficiently
documented must not enter the public release.
